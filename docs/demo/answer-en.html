Surface preparation vs graphene nucleation on copper

Summary

summary
Bottom line

Surface preparation shifts nucleation density by one to two orders of magnitude in reported work. Electropolishing and oxygen pre-treatment both push toward fewer, larger domains.[1,2]

Reading guide

section
  • The table gives reported ranges, not one experiment.
  • Fewer bilayer nuclei usually mean higher final monolayer coverage.
  • Copper melts at 1085 °C; these routes assume a solid foil below that.
